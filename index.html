    How the connections work

    Articles are processed in descending pageview order. A solid line represents an actual Wikipedia hyperlink. A tree uses one connection per branch; the remaining links are cross-links.

    Disconnected groups sit around a shared display center. Dotted spokes are layout guides, not article links. Groups can join when a new article connects them.

    Links include article links supplied by Wikipedia, including links in navigation templates. Topic colors are estimates based on visible categories.

    Rankings cover a single UTC day. Wikimedia provides up to 1,000 ranked pages; non-article pages, duplicates and disambiguation pages are skipped, so fewer articles may be available.
